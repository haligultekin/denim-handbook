# Community & Resource Sharing

A curated list of communities, forums, educators, and makers sharing open knowledge about denim construction and appreciation.

## 💬 Forums & Discussion
- **r/rawdenim:** Reddit's main community for raw and selvedge denim enthusiasts. Great for fade archives and fabric discussions.
- **Superfuture (SuFu):** Historic forum with deep archives on denim evolution, obscure Japanese makers, and construction details.
- **r/sewing:** Broad sewing community that frequently shares denim projects and machine advice.
