# Denim Care & Washing

Considerable misinformation surrounds surrounding how to wash (or not wash) raw denim. Here is the definitive guide to caring for your jeans to maximize longevity and fade contrast.

## The Initial Soak (For Unsanforized Denim Only)
If you purchased or made jeans using *Loomstate (Unsanforized)* denim, you **must** soak them before wearing them.
1. Fill a tub with hot water.
2. Turn the jeans inside out and submerge them for 45-60 minutes (use a heavy bottle to keep them underwater).
3. Hang dry.
*Why?* The hot water removes the starch and causes the fabric to shrink to its final size. If you skip this, sew the jeans, and wash them later, the seams will pucker and the fit will be ruined.

## Routine Washing
If your jeans smell or are visibly dirty, **wash them**. Dirt and grit act like sandpaper inside the fibers, causing crotch blowouts and premature tearing. 
1. **Turn them inside out:** This protects the indigo on the surface from the abrasive washing machine drum.
2. **Cold water & gentle cycle:** Prevents excessive indigo loss.
3. **Use a dark wash detergent:** Avoid bleach or harsh chemicals (like Woolite Dark).
4. **Hang dry:** Never put raw denim in a mechanical dryer. It destroys the leather patch and causes bizarre, unnatural shrinkage patterns.

## Analysis of Common Maintenance Practices
- **"Put them in the freezer to kill bacteria."** 
  - *False.* Freezers do not kill the bacteria that causes odor; they just put it to sleep. Once the jeans warm back up on your body, the smell returns. Wash your jeans.
- **"Ocean washing with sand is the best way to fade."**
  - *False.* While it makes for a interesting visual, salt water and sand degrade cotton fibers rapidly. If you ocean wash, you must thoroughly rinse them with fresh water immediately after.
- **"Never wash them for 6 months."**
  - *Debatable.* Delaying the first wash *does* create sharper, higher-contrast fades (because the indigo rubs off on the creases before water can wash it out evenly). However, waiting too long allows sweat and dirt to rot the cotton, drastically reducing the lifespan of the jeans.
