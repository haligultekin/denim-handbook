# The Ultimate Denim Care Guide

There's a lot of myth and mystery out there about how to properly care for raw denim. This guide is here to break down the facts on how to clean, maintain, and preserve your favorite jeans, so you can get the best possible fades while keeping the fabric strong for years to come.

## The Pre-Soak (For Unsanforized/Loomstate Denim Only)
If your new jeans are made from unsanforized (often called "loomstate") denim, you absolutely need to give them a soak before you start wearing or hemming them! 
1. Fill a tub with hot water.
2. Turn your jeans inside out and fully submerge them for 45 to 60 minutes.
3. Take them out and hang them up to air dry.

*Why do this?* A hot soak removes the stiff industrial starches left over from the weaving process and allows the cotton fibers to shrink to their final size (sometimes up to 10%!). Skipping this step before getting them hemmed or wearing them hard will lead to weird seam puckering and a totally ruined fit once you finally do wash them.

## Routine Washing and Maintenance
Letting dirt, grit, sweat, and oils build up in the fabric acts like microscopic sandpaper, which is the number one cause of dreaded crotch blowouts! When your jeans start to smell or look visibly dirty, it's time to wash them.

1. **Turn them inside out:** This protects the beautiful indigo dye on the outside from rubbing against the inside of the washing machine.
2. **Wash on cold:** Use a cold-water cycle to prevent too much indigo loss and to avoid any unwanted extra shrinking.
3. **Keep the soap mild:** Use a gentle, non-bleach detergent. Harsh chemicals will strip the indigo dye way too fast.
4. **Always hang dry:** Never put your raw denim in the dryer! The intense heat and tumbling can ruin your leather patch and cause the fabric to shrink in weird, unnatural ways.

## Busting Common Denim Myths

- **The Freezer Trick:** 
  - *The Myth:* Throwing your jeans in the freezer kills odor-causing bacteria. 
  - *The Reality:* Freezing just puts the bacteria to sleep. As soon as your jeans warm back up to room temperature, the bacteria wake right up and the smell comes back. A proper wash is the only way to actually clean them!

- **Ocean Washing:**
  - *The Myth:* Wearing your jeans into the ocean gives them incredible fades and a natural wash.
  - *The Reality:* While it's a fun idea, salt water and sand are highly abrasive and will aggressively wear down the cotton fibers. If you decide to do an ocean wash, make sure you immediately rinse the jeans out thoroughly with lots of fresh water to get all the salt and sand out.

- **Waiting Six Months to Wash:**
  - *The Myth:* You must wait at least six months before your first wash to get good fades.
  - *The Reality:* Holding off on washing *will* give you higher-contrast fades, because the indigo rubs off the crease lines before water can wash the dye out evenly. However, avoiding washing means you're letting dirt and sweat eat away at the fabric, which will make your jeans break down and rip much faster. It's a balance between sharp fades and the lifespan of your jeans!
