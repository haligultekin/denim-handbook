# Sewing Machines for Denim

While you can sew lightweight denim on a domestic machine, medium to heavyweight denim generally requires specialized or industrial machinery.

## 🏭 The Core Machines
1. **Lockstitch (Straight Stitch):** The primary workhorse. Look for "needle-feed" or "walking-foot" mechanisms to handle thick layers without skipping stitches.
2. **Overlock (Serger):** Essential for finishing raw edges on non-selvedge denim, or for finishing the inseam of jeans.
3. **Chainstitch:** Uses a looper instead of a bobbin. Famously used for the waistband and the classic "roped" hem (e.g., the legendary Union Special 43200G).
4. **Bartack:** A dedicated machine to sew dense zig-zag stitches at high-stress points like belt loops, crotch seams, and pocket corners.
5. **Buttonhole Machine:** specialized machine that cuts and sews the traditional "keyhole" buttonholes.

## 🏠 Tips for Domestic Machines
- Always use a heavy-duty "Jeans" or "Denim" needle (Size 90/14 to 110/18).
- Use a "Jean-a-ma-jig" or bulky seam jumper to keep the presser foot level when crossing thick flat-felled seams.
- Go very slow over thick seams (like the hem intersecting the inseam). Hand-crank the wheel if necessary to prevent needle breakage.
